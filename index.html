With statements from the Green Party stating they are against animals kept in captivity except for, "genuine conservation or sanctuary purposes," the debate surrounding zoos in the UK has sparked once more.

This debate surrounding zoos has been ongoing for decades, with animal rights activists like Born Free and PETA raising concerns regarding the conservation benefits zoos have, as well as worries surrounding profits from zoos and welfare of the animals kept in captivity.

Through examining the species kept in 6 zoos from the British and Irish Association of Zoos and Aquaria, or BIAZA, an assocciation which strives to upkeep high welfare and conservation within the members, as well as a random sample of over 80,000 animals from the IUCN Red List, and interviews with zoos and anti-captivity groups, we examine the debate in three aspects: whether zoos have as much conservation value as claimed, welfare concerns raised for captive animals, and the financial transparency of zoos.
